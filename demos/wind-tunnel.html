01 · PROBE RAKE

Every sensor is a probe, named and numbered.

In a tunnel you never trust one reading; you read a rake. AVOFLARE reads the engine the same way: each probe streams over the CAN bus into ETPR, and each one is compared with what the healthy twin expects at that moment.
