AVOFLARE

    An atlas of one mission.

    Seven plates from a two-hour flight of a MALE UAV. Each plate is a live scene with a caption that reads the engine at that moment against its healthy twin. Hover a plate to look closer.

      About this structure · Cloud Atlas Plates

      • Sky Atlas world, atlas structure: the page is a bound set of numbered plates, like the International Cloud Atlas, with a contents page, hairline plate frames and registration corners.
      • Every plate is live: the clip plays and its caption values update with the matching stretch of the simulated mission. A frosted magnifier follows your pointer over each plate.
      • Navigation is by plate number (top bar), so each chapter has a stable address, a raise kept from the teletext challenger.
