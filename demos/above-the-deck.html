About this structure · Above the Deck

  • Sky Atlas world, multi-plane structure: the far sky (your cloud video) moves slowest, a mid cloud layer faster, near clouds fastest, with the pointer and with scroll. Chapters hang inside the deck as cloud-glass panels.
  • The clouds here are generated in the browser from soft puffs. The real build would add a clean cut-out of your UAV as its own mid layer, plus cloud layers matted from the stills.
  • Kept from the declined challengers: the fault's number grows with the divergence; panels lift when you point at them.
