3 333 m · CLIMB

Thin air, a working engine.

Outside air drops below −7 °C. Every sensor leaves the engine as an 8-byte CAN frame; ETPR validates, decodes and stamps each value into one CanonicalTelemetryState, the single trusted snapshot the Edge AI reads.

4 000 m · CRUISE

One hour in, the exhaust runs warm.

The simulation starts wearing the turbocharger compressor. Exhaust gas climbs above what the twin expects, crosses the watch band and gets flagged. The engine stays within its limits; the trend is what matters.

CLOUD DECK · LINK LOST

Alone above the clouds. Still deciding.

The downlink drops. Nothing on board stops: the Edge AI keeps judging the engine against its twin and stores what it would have sent. Losing the link costs the ground's extra depth, never the aircraft's own judgement.
